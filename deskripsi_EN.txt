[SIZE=5][COLOR=#A66A00][B]About The Plugin[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]

OrbitSkills is an RPG progression plugin for Minecraft servers. Players do not only level up; they build a character through Stat Points, Race, Clan, Class Rank, fragments, level rewards, and interactive menus.

Each system has its own role:

[LIST]
[*][COLOR=#1E6F9F]Level[/COLOR] is the player's main progression.
[*][COLOR=#237A4B]Stat Points[/COLOR] are used to increase damage, defense, HP, and healing.
[*][COLOR=#5B3F99]Race[/COLOR] is the player's character origin/species. Races can give stats, special effects, or passives.
[*][COLOR=#9A5A00]Clan[/COLOR] is the player's group, bloodline, or guild. Clans focus on stat bonuses without extra passives.
[*][COLOR=#A33A55]Class Rank[/COLOR] is the quality of each stat, from F to SSS.
[*][COLOR=#1E6F9F]Fragments[/COLOR] are used to reroll Race, Clan, and Class Rank.
[/LIST]


[SIZE=5][COLOR=#A66A00][B]Main Features[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*]EXP from many activities such as mobs, mining, farming, fishing, combat, brewing, smelting, raids, and more.
[*]6 main stats: Archery, Fighting, Power, Defense, Health, and Alchemy.
[*]Races with rarity, roll chance, passives, and reroll fragments.
[*]Clans as simple stat bonuses that are easy for players to understand.
[*]Class Rank from F to SSS to make builds more unique.
[*]Auto reroll until the selected rarity is reached.
[*]Guaranteed rarity to protect players from endless bad luck.
[*]Scaled HP so large RPG health values like 4000+ still feel natural.
[*]Actionbar HP and EXP progress.
[*]Leaderboard, profile, guide, level reward, stats, race, clan, and class menus.
[*]PlaceholderAPI support for scoreboards, tabs, and other menus.
[/LIST]


[SIZE=5][COLOR=#A66A00][B]Why OrbitSkills Stands Out[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*]Players do not only grind EXP; they build a full character.
[*]Race gives character identity, while Clan gives another stat direction.
[*]Class Rank makes builds depend on more than just point totals.

[SIZE=5][COLOR=#A66A00][B]Main Commands[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]

[LIST]
[*]/skills, /stats - Open the stats menu.
[*]/level - Open the level menu.
[*]/race - Open the race menu.
[*]/clan - Open the clan menu.
[*]/profile [player] - Show a player profile.
[*]/os help - Admin help.
[*]/os reload - Reload plugin files.
[*]/os scanall [fix] - Check or fix player SP.
[*]/os give point|level|exp <player|*> <amount>
[*]/os give fragment <fragment> <player|*> <amount>
[*]/os set level|race|clan <player> <value>
[*]/os set class [player] <stat> <F-SSS>
[*]/os disable/enable <stats|class|race|clan|all>
[/LIST]
